## What is Wrenchr?

Wrenchr was built out of a simple, universal frustration: **getting ripped off by auto repair shops.**

When your car breaks down or needs a simple repair—like a new tire, brake pads, or a California smog check—you are immediately at an information disadvantage. Mechanics quote wildly different prices, Yelp reviews can be deceiving, and dealerships routinely charge 40% to 70% markups for identical labor.

**Wrenchr solves this instantly.**

By inputting your vehicle's Year, Make, Model, and ZIP code, Wrenchr executes real-time scans across surrounding repair facilities. It parses Google Places, Yelp Fusion, Better Business Bureau (BBB) accreditation, and California Bureau of Automotive Repair (CARB) databases to compute three distinct, unbiased options:

1. **Cheapest Option**: The lowest verified quote in your immediate geographic radius.
2. **Sweet Spot (Best Value)**: An algorithmic blend of 60% trust rating and 40% price savings.
3. **Highest Rated Option**: The shop with the highest composite customer sentiment score.

## Key Product Highlights for Press & Pitching

- **Zero Mock Data Policy**: Built with real-time API integrations and HTTP scrapers with rate-limiting human jitter to ensure 100% genuine data.
- **California CARB / Smog Compliant**: Dedicated logic for California drivers who require Bureau of Automotive Repair (BAR) certified test-only facilities.
- **ASE Certification Vetting**: Highlighting master technician credentials so drivers don't trade safety for price.
- **Built with Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, & Prisma ORM**: Fully deployable to Vercel, Netlify, or AWS with zero tech debt.
